# Imp: launch notes (drafts, edit to your voice)

## One line
Every Imp closes from exactly one point of view. Move, and it comes apart.

## Description
Imp is a hive of square beams that meets at every joint on screen and cannot be built.
The contradiction hides in depth, along the one axis the camera cannot see.
Drag to look from the side and the false joints separate. Let go and the piece is whole, and impossible, again.

Controls: drag to look around. Space or double-click to reveal. R to replay the reveal. D for dusk. F for fullscreen.

## Posts
1. Teaser: screen recording, 6 to 8 s. Hold still 2 s, drag slowly, let it snap back. No caption beyond "it only closes from here."
2. Process: the resting view next to the opened view of the same seed. "Same object. Same instant."
3. Range: a contact sheet crop of 9 pieces, three palettes or more.
4. Mint day: the one line, the date, the link.
